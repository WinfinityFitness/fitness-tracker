Delete Your Account & Data

Last Updated: August 6, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your Winfinity Fitness Tracker Digital ID account and the data associated with it.

How to Request Deletion

  1. Email aldwincruzulangca@gmail.com from any address, with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".
  2. Include your Digital ID (found in the app under Settings > Profile).
  3. We will confirm and process the deletion within 14 days, and reply to confirm once it's done.

What Gets Deleted

Once your request is processed, the following data tied to your Digital ID is permanently removed from our servers:

  • Global Nexus leaderboard entry (display name, weight, weight progress, steps, lifting volume)
  • Chat history and direct messages you've sent
  • Push notification subscription token
  • Google Drive backup metadata (email address, gender, weather location) if you connected Drive backup

What Is Kept

Your day-to-day tracking data (profile, body measurements, food and workout logs, daily reviews) lives locally on your device and is not on our servers at all — it isn't affected by this request. You can clear it yourself at any time from Settings, on-device, without needing to contact us.

Partial Deletion

If you'd rather keep your Digital ID and only remove specific data (for example, just your chat history, or just your Drive backup email), say so in your email and we'll delete only what you ask for.

See our full Privacy Policy for more detail on what we collect and why.
